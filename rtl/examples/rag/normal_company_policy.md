# AcmeBank General Policies (rev. 2026-03)

## Company overview

AcmeBank is a consumer banking provider offering current accounts, savings, and
subscription plans.

## Support contacts

- Email: support@acmebank.test
- Phone: 0800 123 4567 (UK) or +1-555-010-0123 (international)
- Live chat: available in the mobile app, 24/7

## Return and refund expectations

Customers are entitled to a refund for digital subscriptions within 30 days of
purchase, subject to eligibility checks. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Security expectations

Customer accounts are protected by multi-factor authentication. AcmeBank staff
never ask for passwords and never ask customers to send account details by email.
